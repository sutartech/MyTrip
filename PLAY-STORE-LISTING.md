# MyTrip — Google Play listing

**App name (30 max):** MyTrip – Group Trip Planner

**Short description (80 max):**
Plan trips together: shared itinerary, notes, photos and split expenses.

**Full description:**
MyTrip keeps your whole group on the same page — from the first plan to the final settle-up.

PLAN TOGETHER
• Day-by-day itinerary with times, remarks and done ticks
• Drag to reorder, edit rows in place, print or save as PDF
• Today screen shows what's next with a live countdown

SHARE WITH EVERYONE
• Pinned sticky note everyone on the trip can read
• The Administrator chooses who can edit
• Trip photos, places and experiences in one place

SPLIT EXPENSES FAIRLY
• Record who paid for what
• Share costs by person or by family
• Settle up shows the fewest payments needed — shown when the Administrator decides

MADE FOR GROUPS
• One Administrator controls trips, travellers and features
• Hide or order trips for everyone
• Profile photos for each traveller
• Auto sign-out: 5 min, 30 min, 1 hr or 2 hr

SIMPLE AND FAST
• Clean, calm design with dark mode
• Larger text option
• Opens instantly and works on slow connections
• Built-in Help & FAQ, and ratings to suggest new features

No ads. Your trip data stays private to your group.
