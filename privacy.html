MYTRIP

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

MyTrip helps small groups plan trips, share an itinerary, keep notes and track shared expenses. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information we store

  • Traveller names, Traveller IDs and profile photos added by you or the trip Administrator.
  • Trip details: itinerary plans, remarks, places, sticky notes, experiences and trip photos.
  • Expenses: amount, description and who paid.
  • Ratings and suggestions you choose to send through Help & Feedback.

Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, never as plain text.

Where it is stored

Trip data is kept in a private Google Sheet and Google Drive folder owned by the trip Administrator, and processed through Google Apps Script. On your device, the app keeps your sign-in for the auto sign-out period chosen by the Administrator, and a copy of the open trip so it loads quickly and works offline.

How it is used

Information is used only to show the trip to its members and to run app features. We do not sell data, show ads or share information with third parties for marketing.

Who can see it

Only the Administrator and the travellers added to a trip can see that trip. The Administrator can control which features each traveller may view or edit.

Weather

To show a forecast, the trip destination name (not your location) is sent to Open-Meteo, a free weather service.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time to remove your sign-in and saved trip copy from the device.
  • Ask the trip Administrator to change or delete your details, photos or entries.
  • To delete a whole trip or account, contact us at the address below.

Children

MyTrip is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
